The Federal University of Pampa is releasing its Citizen Services Charter, a document
that brings together, in a clear and accessible way, the services the institution
provides to the community.

The charter presents its service commitments, contact channels and the information
students, staff and the general public need to access the university's public services.

The initiative is part of the university's transparency efforts and its ongoing work to
improve service to citizens.
