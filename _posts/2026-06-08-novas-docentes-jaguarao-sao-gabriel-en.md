The Jaguarão and São Gabriel campuses of the Federal University of Pampa have welcomed
new faculty members, who were sworn in and now join the institution's permanent teaching
staff.

The appointments strengthen the undergraduate programs as well as the research and
outreach activities carried out at the two units.

The university welcomes its new staff and reaffirms its commitment to the quality of
public education across the ten cities where it operates.
