The Energy Engineering program at the Federal University of Pampa will offer, for the
first time, an evening class starting in the second semester of 2026.

The move widens access to public higher education for students who need to balance work
and study, meeting a long-standing demand from the community.

Places will be filled through the university's regular selection processes. Information
on enrollment and the academic calendar is published in the Admissions section.
