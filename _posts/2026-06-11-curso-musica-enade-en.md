The Music program at the Federal University of Pampa achieved the fourth-best national
ranking in the National Student Performance Exam (ENADE), behind only USP, UFBA and
UNICAMP.

The result reflects the qualification of the teaching staff and the dedication of the
students, and it consolidates Unipampa's place among the best programs in the field in
Brazil.

The program's leadership noted that the achievement reinforces the university's role in
training professionals and in the region's cultural production.
