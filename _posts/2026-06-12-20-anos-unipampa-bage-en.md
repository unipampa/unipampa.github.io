The Federal University of Pampa celebrated its 20th anniversary with a special program
at the Bagé Campus, home to the Rectory. The celebrations brought together the academic
community, public officials and residents of the Campanha region.

Among the highlights was the inauguration of a new academic building, which expands the
institution's teaching and research infrastructure. In the evening, concerts and
cultural activities, open to the public, marked the date.

Founded in 2005, Unipampa was created with the mission of bringing free, quality public
higher education to the interior of Rio Grande do Sul, and it is present today in ten
cities.
