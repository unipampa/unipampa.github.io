Professor Edward Pessano, of the Federal University of Pampa, has been elected
vice-president of the Border Universities Network (Unifronteiras), which brings together
institutions operating in border regions.

The appointment recognizes Unipampa's work on academic integration and cooperation,
given its strategic location on Brazil's border with Uruguay and Argentina.

The network promotes joint teaching, research and outreach initiatives among its member
universities.
